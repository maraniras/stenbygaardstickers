## Fælles prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ONE collectible PROFILE AVATAR FRAME for a mobile app, front-facing and perfectly circular, square canvas. IMPORTANT: this is only an ornamental RING, not a medal, not a badge, not a picture: the entire center is a completely empty perfectly circular TRANSPARENT hole, diameter 64% of canvas, centered at (50%,50%). No face, avatar, placeholder, colored center, black center, white center, background or floor. TRUE alpha transparency both through the hole and outside the frame, including small gaps between decorations. The frame forms a continuous polished sculpted 3D ring around that empty hole; all decorations stay on the rim, never inside the central hole. Outer shape contained within 94% of canvas, no cropped tips. Premium playful colorful detailed 3D emoji/toy-like rendering, rounded bevels, tactile glossy materials, soft front studio highlights, readable at small size. No text, letters, numbers, watermark or enclosing square.

## Motiver

- **bananbande**: Bananbande. The circular rim is formed from several ripe curved golden-yellow bananas intertwined with emerald tropical leaves; subtle warm amber metallic inner edging. A tiny banana blossom and a couple of small glossy leaf clusters accent the bottom, asymmetrical but balanced, fun and friendly. Bananas follow the circumference of the ring and do not cross the central hole. No crown, no currency.
- **casino-royal**: Casino Royal. A refined warm metallic gold circular ring with sculpted red and charcoal black poker-chip segments embedded around the circumference, tasteful tiny ivory roulette-ball details, a compact red-and-black roulette-pocket ornament at the top and restrained gold art-deco flourishes at the bottom. No digits, letters, card suits in the center, face or casino scenery. Clear visible difference from a Christmas gift frame.
- **frostklar**: Frostklar. A circular sculpted frosty ice rim in luminous pale cyan, royal blue and pearl white, with rounded translucent crystal facets, miniature six-point snowflakes and soft snowy tips around the perimeter. Elegant restrained silvery inner trim, a few small icy sparkles close to the ring. Winter ice theme, not Christmas: no fir, gifts, baubles or Santa hats. Central hole remains entirely transparent.
- **galaktisk**: Galaktisk. A deep violet and indigo cosmic circular rim, soft luminous magenta and blue nebula-like material constrained to the solid ring, a handful of small sculpted gold stars and one tiny orbiting ringed planet on the outer lower-right edge. Glossy toy-like space fantasy, tasteful thin gold inner edge. No haze, starfield or nebula inside the empty transparent circular hole or across the outer transparent canvas.
