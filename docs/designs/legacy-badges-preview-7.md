## Fælles prompt og referencer

Use case: stylized-concept. One standalone 3D achievement icon. Identity reference: `branding/logo.png`, recognizable brown-haired shouting Emil profile printed on a cream-edged sticker. Style/material reference only: `legacy-badges-preview-6/likes.png`. Do not copy its composition. Rich glossy purple enamel, warm gold/brass, cream and mint accents, tactile polished playful 3D, soft studio lighting. Bold compact silhouette readable at 64px, one face total. Square, fully contained with 8% transparent padding, genuine alpha. No background rectangle, floor, outer medal ring, writing, digits, watermark. One image_gen call per asset, transparent_background=true.
