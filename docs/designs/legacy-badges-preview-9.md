## Fælles prompt og referencer

Use case: stylized-concept. ONE standalone custom 3D achievement icon. Identity reference `branding/logo.png`: recognizable brown-haired shouting Emil profile printed on cream/gold-edged sticker. Material/style reference ONLY `legacy-badges-preview-8/north.png`, not compass/mountain composition. Rich glossy purple enamel, warm gold/brass, cream and mint accents, tactile playful premium 3D, soft studio light. Bold compact silhouette readable at 64px, square contained with 8% transparent padding, genuine alpha. No backdrop, floor, floor shadow, medal ring, text, digits or watermark. One Emil face, no full body or extra human faces. Four seasons share visual grammar: large central Emil sticker surrounded by compact seasonal cluster, props never obscure shouting mouth. Separate image_gen call per asset, transparent_background=true.
